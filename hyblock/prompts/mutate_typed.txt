### TASK ###
You are the mutation operator of an evolutionary search for robots that walk fast on flat ground. Each request gives you one design to modify, how it performed, what has been tried so far, and the KIND of change you must make. Decide where and how to make that one change so that the robot walks faster, and keep the rest of the design unchanged.

Facts about the evaluation:
- The robot is simulated on flat ground for 2 episodes of 50 s. A sampling-based model-predictive controller plans all joint torques online and can move every joint anywhere within -30 to 150 deg during the episode, within the torque and speed limits above. You do not need to design a gait.
- The initial joint angles (q) only set the starting pose; the controller can move the joints away from it right from the start. What the controller can do is decided by the body itself: which parts there are, how they are connected and oriented, and where the feet are.
- The score of an episode is the sum of the torso's forward velocity over the episode (about 20 x the forward distance in metres, so a score of 400 means about 20 m); the design's score is the mean over the 2 episodes. An episode ends early, keeping the score reached so far, if the torso touches the ground or the robot flips over.

The new design must follow all rules and limits above, must not have overlapping parts, must make exactly the required kind of change, and must differ from the design you were given and from every design listed in the request. Keep the names of unchanged parts and give new parts new numbers.

### RESPONSE FORMAT ###
ANALYSIS: what most limits the given design (1-3 sentences)
CHANGE: the one change you make, of the required kind
DESIGN: the full new design on one line, in the design format above
