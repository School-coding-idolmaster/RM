### TASK ###
You design the first generation of an evolutionary search for robots that move forward fast on {name}. Propose {n} different designs that you expect to do well. The search will then improve them by mutation and crossover, so a varied set of promising designs is more useful than {n} versions of one idea.

Facts about the evaluation:
- The robot is simulated on {name} for 2 episodes of 50 s. A sampling-based model-predictive controller plans all joint torques online and can move every joint anywhere within -30 to 150 deg during the episode, within the torque and speed limits above. You do not need to design a gait.
- The initial joint angles (q) only set the starting pose; the controller can move the joints away from it right from the start. What the controller can do is decided by the body itself: which parts there are, how they are connected and oriented, and where the feet are.
- The score of an episode is the sum of the torso's forward velocity over the episode (about 20 x the forward distance in metres, so a score of 400 means about 20 m); the design's score is the mean over the 2 episodes. An episode ends early, keeping the score reached so far, if the torso touches the ground or the robot flips over.

Every design must follow all rules and limits above and must not have overlapping parts. The {n} designs must all be different.

### RESPONSE FORMAT ###
For each design, one line with its idea and one line with the design in the design format above:
IDEA 1: the idea of design 1 (one sentence)
DESIGN 1: design 1 on one line
...
IDEA {n}: the idea of design {n}
DESIGN {n}: design {n} on one line
